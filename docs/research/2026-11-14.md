## 2. Specs

- **VRRP:** RFC 5798 is obsoleted by **RFC 9568** (2024-04-30). Docs and
  `src/vrrp.rs` cite 5798; 9568 is a clarification/errata roll-up. Worth a
  read-through against the state machine, and a citation change. No 2026
  technical change. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9568/history/
- **BGP:** `draft-ietf-idr-bgp4-rfc4271bis-02` (2026-10-07) is still a draft;
  WG milestone to IESG May 2027. It folds in RFC 6286 (AS-wide unique BGP
  Identifier), 6608, 8212, 9687. Nothing to act on yet; #6's OPEN
  validation follows 4271. https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-idr-bgp4-rfc4271bis/
